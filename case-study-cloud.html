01 / The challenge

Large migrations are systems programs, not file transfers.

Moving a large content estate across multiple enterprise brands requires technical coordination, architectural decision-making, and disciplined delivery. Every migration wave must protect data integrity while keeping the broader platform and teams aligned.

02 / Ownership

Architecture, delivery, and operational detail moved together.

Rahul drove technology selection and architectural decisions while spearheading the movement of 50 TB from on-premises servers across 15 brands. The destination was integrated with Adobe AEM, with Agile delivery practices used to keep a complex program moving in controlled increments.

  • Technical planning across a multi-brand enterprise environment.
  • Architecture and technology decisions aligned with the target platform.
  • Migration delivery structured around traceable, incremental progress.
  • Integration with Adobe AEM as the destination content platform.

03 / Leadership

Calm coordination is an engineering capability.

Alongside the migration, Rahul held ownership of mission-critical Payment, Banking, Revenue, and BOD/EOD modules. The work strengthened his approach to balancing delivery risk, architectural clarity, and the operational realities of enterprise systems.

Outcome

50 TB of data moved across 15 enterprise brands into a cloud-integrated content platform.
