# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary visitors are engineering leaders, hiring managers, recruiters, and senior engineers evaluating Rahul Kumar for senior backend, platform, reliability, and distributed-systems roles. Secondary visitors are engineers who discover Rahul through his writing and want to understand his practical experience, projects, and point of view.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Rahul's engineering work, career progression, technical depth, and writing in one credible narrative. A successful visit gives a first-time visitor a clear understanding of what Rahul builds, the scale and impact of his work, and an obvious route to read a case study, review his resume, explore his writing, or start a conversation.

## Positioning

Rahul combines eight-plus years of production backend experience with platform engineering, cloud reliability, developer tooling, and a growing body of practical technical writing. The portfolio must connect measurable enterprise outcomes to the engineering judgment behind them.

## Operating Context

The site is a static, responsive portfolio hosted under the `/personal-portfolio/` GitHub Pages path. It is commonly reviewed quickly on desktop or mobile, shared through social previews, and used as a bridge to Rahul's resume, GitHub, LinkedIn, Topmate, and The System Design Atlas.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Keep the site dependency-light and compatible with the existing Node 24 static build and validation workflow.
- Preserve the homepage, three case-study routes, resume link, SEO metadata, sitemap, robots policy, and GitHub Pages base-path behavior.
- Motion must be purposeful, pausable, and fully respectful of `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- All factual claims must come from the existing portfolio, resume-derived copy, case studies, or Rahul's published sites; do not fabricate employers, results, testimonials, audience numbers, or technologies.
- External writing should point to `https://system-design-atlas-theta.vercel.app/` and its real article routes.

## Brand Commitments

- Use Rahul Kumar's name, professional portrait, and real engineering content.
- The voice is calm, precise, technically credible, and direct rather than promotional.
- The reference portfolio at `https://sarthak-sharma-portfolio-six.vercel.app/` is a binding inspiration for editorial pacing, portrait-led storytelling, fixed navigation, and restrained scroll motion, but the result must be original to Rahul rather than a pixel-for-pixel copy.

## Evidence on Hand

- Professional portrait assets in `assets/images/portfolio/rahul-professional*`.
- Resume at `assets/docs/Rahul_Kumar_Resume_JPMC.pdf`.
- Case studies for CI/CD platform engineering, resilient MCP foundations, and a 50 TB cloud migration.
- Current career timeline covering JPMorgan Chase & Co., Oracle, and Mindtree.
- Published writing and learning paths at The System Design Atlas, including real article URLs and topic counts.
- Existing social cards, structured data, responsive image variants, and automated quality checks.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the engineer and his point of view, then prove it with specific work.
2. Make measurable impact easier to scan than a list of technologies.
3. Let motion create narrative focus without delaying access to content.
4. Treat writing as evidence of engineering depth, not a side project.
5. Keep every interaction fast, accessible, and dependable on GitHub Pages.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The experience must remain keyboard navigable, preserve visible focus states and semantic headings, provide meaningful image alternatives, keep content readable without JavaScript, support a motion-off control, and honor operating-system reduced-motion preferences.
