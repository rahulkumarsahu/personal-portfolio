# Homepage Direction — The Engineering Signal Journal

## Decision

The user's reference fixes the visual direction: a portrait-led, cinematic editorial portfolio with restrained motion. The concept roll assigned the fourth grounded candidate, which is also the closest truthful translation of that reference into Rahul's technical identity.

## Grounded candidates

1. Operations war-room logbook — incident timelines, runbook annotations, and calm control-room hierarchy.
2. System-design field atlas — architectural plates, measured diagrams, and engineer's notebook pacing.
3. Financial engineering annual report — rigorous typography, material metrics, and institutional proof.
4. Engineering signal journal — large editorial portrait, serif narrative voice, disciplined grid, and signal-green technical annotations.
5. Punch-card service catalog — modular records, tabular metadata, and library-like discovery.
6. Transit control schematic — routes, transfers, and operational states as the navigation language.
7. Oscilloscope archive — dark lab surfaces, measured traces, and time-based system behavior.

## World

An independent engineering journal built in charcoal, warm ivory, and one electric signal green. Rahul's professional portrait is the decisive image. Oversized editorial serif type carries the human story; Manrope carries readable explanation; mono is reserved for dates, measurements, code, and system states.

## First viewport

A floating framed navigation sits above a full-height portrait composition. Rahul's image occupies the center like a magazine cover, while precise annotations establish role, location, and current focus. His name spans the lower half in large serif type, followed by one plain statement of value and two direct actions. The image responds subtly to pointer and scroll movement.

## Visitor path

Presence → measurable proof → selected systems → engineering approach → career progression → working expertise → published thinking → direct contact.

## Signature interaction

Scroll behaves like a focus rack: section statements arrive from soft blur into sharp clarity while project covers reveal through a vertical mask. A persistent motion control pauses the system and reduced-motion preferences disable it automatically.

## Cross-surface reach

Case studies can inherit the journal's serif scale, ruled metadata, warm paper moments, signal-green state language, and image-led chapter openings without duplicating the homepage composition.

## Honest risk

Editorial drama can make technical evidence harder to scan. The design counters this with short copy, explicit metrics, real project outcomes, semantic structure, and predictable actions.

## Challenger verdicts and raises

- Rhythm machine: declined on audience identification and clarity. Kept discipline: motion runs as one timed sequence rather than scattered effects.
- ASCII live scene: competitive on engineering recognition, weaker on personal warmth. Kept discipline: one technical image treatment, never site-wide terminal cosplay.
- Multiplane dawn: declined on audience identification. Kept discipline: layered depth and focus-rack motion with a reduced mobile form.
- Forged iron: declined because the metaphor overpowers Rahul's actual work. Kept discipline: before/after outcomes stay visibly measurable.
- Mid-century magazine program: competitive on both axes and closest to the pinned reference. Kept discipline: one strict editorial grid and a single accent per view.
- Swiss poster wall: competitive on product clarity. Kept discipline: every section snaps to a reusable module rather than inventing a new card pattern.

## Build path

Code-led for this session because the user supplied a concrete live visual reference and an established static implementation. This session choice is not stored as a project default.
